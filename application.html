Sell what you grow, bake or make.

Midrand Farmers Market is dedicated to giving small farmers, brands and artists a platform, whilst also being a pillar for community. The market is located at 15 Douglas Road, Glen Austin, and operates every Sunday, 09:00-15:30.

PLEASE NOTE

What we don't allow

Because we are a farmers market, we can't allow everyone to sell anything. We do not allow items from Shein, Alibaba/AliExpress, Temu, or AI-generated products.

PLEASE NOTE

Staying on our books

Our traders need to trade with us at least once per month to stay on our books.

PLEASE NOTE

What you need to bring

Your own folding table, chair, gazebo or sturdy umbrella with stand, and stall weights for wind. You'll also need signage - let us know if you need assistance with this.

PLEASE NOTE

Food & fresh produce stalls

Must strictly have a current COA certificate from the City of Johannesburg.
