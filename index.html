About Us

A market rooted in the community.

Midrand Farmers Market was founded in pursuit of building community and giving makers and growers, who thoughtfully produce their products, a platform.

Surrounded by nature, our market is the perfect place to reset and unwind. Perfectly balanced - our space allows for shoppers to carefully stroll from stall to stall, families to enjoy a picnic whilst watching their kids enjoy a number of fun activities, and dog owners to peacefully walk their dog along a river trail.

Bring your family, bring a blanket, have a picnic and catch a glimpse of a cow or two.
