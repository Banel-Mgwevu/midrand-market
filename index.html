About Us

A market rooted in the community.

Midrand Farmers Market is a monthly gathering of local growers, bakers, roasters and craftspeople, held right here in Midrand. We started this market to give small, independent producers a place to sell directly to the people who'll enjoy what they make — no middlemen, no mass production, just good food and honest craft.

Every stall is hand-picked. Expect seasonal vegetables and fruit, farm eggs, artisan bread, small-batch preserves, coffee, plants and handmade goods, alongside food trucks and live music that turn the morning into a proper outing for the whole family.

We're proudly outdoor, dog-friendly, and pram-friendly — come early for the best pick of the produce, and stay for a slow Sunday morning with good coffee in hand.
