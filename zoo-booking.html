Zoo Booking

Book the petting zoo corner.

A small, gentle animal encounter for kids, running every market Sunday. Slots are limited, so reserve ahead to guarantee your time.

What to expect

Our petting zoo corner is a calm, supervised space where children can meet and feed friendly farm animals - rabbits, goats, chickens and guinea pigs - while parents browse the stalls nearby.

  • Suitable for children of all ages, supervised throughout
  • Hand-sanitising stations at entry and exit
  • 30-minute time slots to keep groups small
  • Animal feed included in the booking
